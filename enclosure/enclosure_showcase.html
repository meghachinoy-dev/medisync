The three prints

What prints, and how

One monolithic body plus two small removable parts. The lid and tray can't be fused — you lift the lid to fill compartments and pull the tray out to collect pills.

body

150 × 101 × 110 mm · 1×

The whole structure in one print: 2×3 compartment tower fused to the electronics base — LCD window, 6 servo pockets, 6 chutes, USB & power ports, side vents, front dispense opening. Slice with internal supports (deck bridges the cavity).

lid

~144 × 95 × 10 mm · 1×

One friction-fit lid over all six bins for filling. Grip ribs + a finger notch to lift. No supports.

tray

60 × 42 × 22 mm · 1×

Pull-out cup that catches dispensed pills at the front opening. Hex handle. No supports.

Build

Assembly order

  1. Through the open bottom, mount the 6 SG90 servos into the deck pockets and route wiring.
  2. Fit the LCD to the front window (M3), NodeMCU by the USB slot, power to the grommet.
  3. Fill the compartments from the top, then press on the lid (friction skirt).
  4. Slide the tray into the front opening to catch dispensed pills.
